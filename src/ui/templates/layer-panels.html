          GLOBAL ABUNDANCE MAP
          
          
        
                                
        
                                
        
                                
          INSTRUCTIONS
          
                                  
            
                                1. Pick the LEVEL: countries, states and provinces, or cities. SHOW ON MAP colours the globe from scarce (red) to abundant (green).
                                  2. 
            
                                2. Tick the INDICATORS that make the score (ALL, or just some). Each row says what it measures at this level and how many places have a figure of their own; a state takes its country's figure where it has none, a city its state's or country's.
                                  3. 
            
                                3. Slide a weight to make an indicator count more in the score. With one indicator ticked the legend shows its real values. The arrows give the change of each figure over five years.
                                  4. 
            
                                4. Rest the pointer on a place to read its figures under SELECTED; click it to keep it there and on the map. RANKING lists the most and least abundant.
                                  5. 
            
                                5. ABUNDANCE GRID draws the first orchestration model: every scarce place linked to its nearest abundant one.
                                  6. 
            
                                6. DATA: the World Bank, Our World in Data and the OECD regional database, fetched by REFRESH DATA and kept thirty days. LOAD CSV puts your own figures on top; CLEAR CSV takes them off.
